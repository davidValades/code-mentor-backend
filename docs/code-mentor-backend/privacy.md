# Code Mentor Backend privacy policy

Responsible person and contact: David Valadés — davidvaladesnavarro@gmail.com.

## Using the skill

Code Mentor Backend provides instructions for learning C# and .NET. Its creator does not automatically collect conversations, code, or learning records and has not added any analytics or data collection services of his own.

If the environment supports it and the user configures it, the assistant can save learning files in a location it can access. Their content, location, access, and retention depend on the platform and the user's configuration. The skill does not automatically synchronize records between devices.

The optional knowledge library uses Markdown cards, an index, and revision history in an agreed location. The assistant proposes changes and requests acceptance before saving them. These files are not automatically published in this repository or sent to the creator.

Do not share credentials or company information you are not authorized to use or save.

The optional adaptation log links teaching aids to previously saved evidence and generates local summaries and charts. Declared preferences and these derived files remain in the authorized location and scope, without being sent automatically to the creator. Deletion requests must also account for these files and their accessible copies.

## Support

You can request help through this repository's [GitHub issues](https://github.com/davidValades/code-mentor-backend/issues). Public issues and their content may be visible to anyone. Do not include passwords, keys, personal data, private conversations, or confidential code. The creator can read and respond to what is posted. GitHub manages its own services and retains or deletes information according to its own policies and controls.

## Private inquiries

For inquiries about data you have provided to the creator, write to davidvaladesnavarro@gmail.com. Inquiries are retained until resolution and for a maximum of 90 days afterward; the responsible person must review and delete the messages manually.

You can request information, correction, or deletion of data you have provided, subject to applicable limits and obligations. The creator does not control data retained by platforms and cannot guarantee its deletion from third-party systems.

## Platforms

ChatGPT, Codex, GitHub, and the email provider may process information according to their own policies. Consult their respective terms and privacy policies.

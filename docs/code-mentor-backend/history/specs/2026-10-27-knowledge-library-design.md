# Personal library and guided study

> Historical translated design record dated October 27, 2026. It preserves the scope agreed at that time and does not establish the current installation or publication state.

Agreed extension of Code Mentor Backend: adapt documentation study to the learner's demonstrated knowledge and maintain Markdown cards outside the package.

## Approaches

Extending the existing memory and curriculum was selected. A separate mode would duplicate progress; session summaries alone would not allow concepts to be retrieved by ID. Cards summarize knowledge and learning; they do not replace log evidence.

## Behavior

- Consult the index, relevant cards, evidence, and checkpoint; do not load the entire library.
- Verify primary sources, URL, consultation date, and applicable version. Design a short route based on demonstrated prerequisites and teach one idea per turn with practice.
- Propose cards at topic or task closure, every three sessions, and after repeated difficulty. Group coinciding triggers; without relevant changes, do not create revisions.
- Show the proposal and request acceptance of its content before writing or updating a card. Prior operational authorization for evidence and checkpoints remains in force.
- Use a stable ID, ISO dates with timezone, incremental revision, source, evidence and assistance level, questions, next practice, and suggested review. A self-report does not demonstrate mastery.
- Use a `conocimientos/` directory with an index, current cards, and revision history outside the skill and public repository; agree on a persistent location, with no company data in the general scope.
- Use file tools for cards without inventing CLI operations. The existing JSON log remains the source of evidence and reviews; do not modify its schema.
- Without access, show a pending draft; no background work, self-training, or implicit synchronization.

## Scope

References and templates, connections from `SKILL.md` and memory/curriculum, a public guide, and package metadata. No actual learner card is published, and no personal log is created in an unagreed location.
